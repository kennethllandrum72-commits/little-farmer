Homestead Adventure

Tools: cycle AXE → PICKAXE → HANDS. Trees need the axe, rocks/mine need the pickaxe, crops need hands.

Stamina: actions use stamina. It slowly recovers and sleeping fully restores it.

Progression: upgrade your barn for more stamina and faster animal production. Upgrade the farm for bigger crop yields and better sale prices.

Fishing: stand in the glowing AUTO FISH circle on the dock. Fishing starts automatically.
