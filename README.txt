LITTLE FARMER - iPhone Version

Files:
- index.html
- manifest.json

To play:
1. Put these files on any simple web host.
2. Open index.html in Safari on your iPhone.
3. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen.

Controls:
- Left joystick: move
- USE: chop trees, mine rocks, harvest crops
- PLANT: plant seeds
- SELL: sell harvested crops
- UPGRADE TOOL: improve tool power

Your progress saves automatically in Safari using local storage.
